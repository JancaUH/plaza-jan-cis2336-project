Frequently Asked Questions

What is ArtConnect?
ArtConnect is a platform that connects artists and art enthusiasts, providing a space to showcase and discover artwork.
How can I submit my artwork?
You can submit your artwork by filling out the submission form on the Artist Submission page.
Are there any fees for submitting artwork?
No, submitting artwork on ArtConnect is completely free.
Can I sell my artwork through ArtConnect?
Yes, you can list your artwork for sale on ArtConnect. Please provide pricing information during the submission process.
What if there are any issues with my submission?
You can contact our support team for assistance with any issues related to your submission.
